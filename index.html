CLARITY INCLUDES THE LIMITS

Grounded in science.
Honest about uncertainty.

Experimental baseline · v0.1

A personal model, not a fixed 28-day rule.

akaflow uses a Bayesian statistical model of your completed cycle lengths. It learns both your typical timing and how much it varies. Its Student-t predictive distribution accounts for uncertainty in the estimated average and variability.

We need at least three eligible completed cycles before showing a forecast. More variable histories produce wider ranges. An 80% range is a statement about the model, not a proven 80% success rate.

What the research supports

Li et al., JAMIA (2021) studied a probabilistic approach that distinguishes cycle patterns from missed tracking. Urteaga et al., MLHC (2021) studied predictive calibration with a more flexible count model. Their research code is public.

These papers motivate personalized, uncertainty-aware prediction. This app's simpler normal–inverse-gamma model is an original baseline, not a reproduction of their trained models. Their performance results do not apply to akaflow.

What we don't know from dates

Period dates alone don't confirm ovulation, hormone levels, or a fertile window. Bull et al. (2019) documented substantial variation in cycle and ovulation timing. We don't draw hormone curves or assign fertility labels from a calendar.

Missing records change the picture

A long interval may represent a long cycle or a period that wasn't recorded. We never silently divide it into assumed cycles. You can exclude an incomplete interval in your period history. If a forecast window has passed, we ask you to review your record rather than silently moving the goalposts.

Who this baseline is for

The intended scope is personal tracking of adult natural cycles. Predictions pause when you select hormonal contraception, pregnancy, postpartum, or another context. The model hasn't been validated for clinical use, adolescents, PCOS, perimenopause, or changing treatment. Symptoms are recorded, but do not influence forecasts.

Before calling it a validated predictor

We would need consented representative data, participant-separated and chronological evaluation, comparison with simple baselines, interval coverage testing, subgroup analysis, and prospective independent validation. No such validation has been performed for this app.

Do not use these predictions to prevent pregnancy or confirm ovulation. ACOG explains fertility awareness methods and their limits.

ALWAYS YOURS

Tracking context

Forecasts are enabled only for natural cycles. After a context change, review and exclude intervals that aren't representative before using predictions.

Stored in your browser

Entries and predictions stay on this device. There is no login, analytics, cloud sync, or external AI service. The hosting provider still receives normal website requests.

Browser storage is not encrypted by this app. People with access to your browser, browser extensions, or other code on the same website origin may be able to read it. Clearing site data removes your records. Private browsing may not preserve them. Use a dedicated hosting origin for sensitive records.

Backup files contain readable health data. Keep them somewhere private. Import replaces the records in this browser after confirmation.

A fresh start

Remove all akaflow records from this browser. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.
