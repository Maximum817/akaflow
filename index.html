CLARITY INCLUDES THE LIMITS

Grounded in science.
Honest about uncertainty.

Published model · browser adaptation v1

A published prediction method, on your device.

akaflow now uses the generalized-Poisson model with missed-record inference from Urteaga et al., MLHC (2021). The authors provide MIT-licensed reference code.

The model allows both narrow and wide cycle-length distributions. A longer interval can reflect a longer cycle, missed tracking, or both; it weighs these possibilities rather than automatically treating every gap as one cycle.

What has been reproduced

The generalized-Poisson likelihood, its finite-support normalization, the truncated-geometric model of missed records, and importance-weighted posterior prediction replace the original Student-t baseline. Calculations run in a background worker without sending your records anywhere.

An important distinction: method versus trained model

This implementation uses the reference repository's default priors, not population parameters fitted on the study cohort. We found no released fitted parameter artifact in the reviewed repository. It learns your individual posterior from your records, but does not reproduce the study's population training or inherit its reported accuracy.

What the date means

We predict the next biological cycle, assuming its next start will be recorded (the reference model's no-future-skip setting). Historical missed entries are still considered during learning. This differs from predicting how long it will take until somebody next opens the app and logs a period.

The displayed range contains at least 80% of the calculated positive-day probability mass. It is not a demonstrated 80% success rate. We check retained probability, sampling effectiveness, and agreement between two sample batches. If those numerical checks fail, we withhold the forecast.

Your records stay yours

Excluded intervals stay excluded. Inferred missed records are never added to your calendar as facts. A forecast that passes remains visible as an old estimate and prompts a record check; it is not silently postponed.

What period dates cannot establish

We do not predict ovulation, fertility, hormone levels, or symptoms. Dates alone cannot confirm those events. Predictions remain paused outside the adult natural-cycle context. The model has not been validated for PCOS, perimenopause, adolescents, postpartum, pregnancy, hormonal contraception, or treatment changes.

How we checked the port

Automated checks compare probability kernels, posterior weights, and predictive distributions with an independent Python equation implementation. Personal backtesting uses earlier records only and compares with a historical median. These tests check implementation behavior; independent prospective clinical validation is still needed.

Tracking context

Forecasts are enabled only for natural cycles. After a context change, review and exclude intervals that aren't representative before using predictions.

Stored in your browser

Entries and predictions stay on this device. There is no login, analytics, cloud sync, or external AI service. The hosting provider still receives normal website requests.

Browser storage is not encrypted by this app. People with access to your browser, browser extensions, or other code on the same website origin may be able to read it. Clearing site data removes your records. Private browsing may not preserve them. Use a dedicated hosting origin for sensitive records.

Backup files contain readable health data. Keep them somewhere private. Import replaces the records in this browser after confirmation.

A fresh start

Remove all akaflow records from this browser. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.
